# Connected respiratory macro-to-micro explorer

The explorer follows one retained route: whole lungs → trachea → patient right/left primary bronchus → lobar bronchus → named segmental bronchus → smaller bronchi → bronchioles → terminal bronchioles → respiratory bronchioles → alveolar ducts → alveolar sacs → individual alveoli → alveolar–capillary membrane. It begins in the existing moving HRA lung scene and uses the existing mechanics clock throughout.

## Source identity and boundary

`assets/airway-metadata.js` is generated by `tools/simulators/derive-breathing.py`. Each of the 29 original airway meshes has its source node/name, original source parent, index start/count, vertex start/count, normalized centroid and bounds recorded. These ranges partition the merged airway exactly. `breathing-data.js` remains byte-for-byte unchanged (SHA-256 `71ef7cfc01436246450ac7dbf80f96d5b9c62c5057fe3c62b8164f4850382894`). Selecting or picking a named airway highlights only its original source index range, using the existing deformed position buffer; no duplicated airway positions or invented source branches are added.

HRA grouping parents identify source hierarchy, not continuous measured airway centerlines. The anatomical hierarchy in `explorer.js` independently associates source named bronchi with side and lobe. The right source mesh called `VH_F_right_main_lobar_bronchus` is presented as the right primary bronchus with its exact source identifier preserved. Bronchopulmonary segments have their own segmental (tertiary) bronchus. Gross mesh registration and physical scale retain the qualifications in `RESPIRATORY-V2-MODEL.md`.

The transition after the named segmental bronchus is explicitly marked **MAGNIFIED INSTRUCTIONAL RECONSTRUCTION**. Distal branches and airspaces are authored representative geometry: they do not trace microscopic HRA continuity, patient anatomy, generation count, a measured airway cast or a clinical reconstruction. The path retains the selected segment identifier, side, lobe and original parent centroid. A second camera renders a small real HRA source-location inset using the same renderer; the original selected source branch is highlighted there independently of general gross-structure selection. Isolation hides source context; restoring layers reveals the same retained segment. A wrapping source-origin caption sits below the canvas. At narrow widths or enlarged text the inset collapses while this caption preserves the full segment and lobe context. Gross patient-direction captions are hidden in authored microscopic views, whose axes are local instructional coordinates.

## Scale

Physical teaching sizes are separated from screen enlargement. `explorer.js` labels approximate illustrative category ranges at gross/small-airway levels. They are not measurements of the HRA mesh, universal cutoffs or an inferred physical calibration. The referenced teaching examples are bronchioles about 1 mm, individual alveoli about 200 µm and respiratory membrane about 0.5 µm. Display magnification varies with viewport and camera zoom; wall and barrier thickness are exaggerated for visibility. No numeric magnification factor is claimed.

## Interaction and state

Selecting a named source bronchus preserves its exact source identity. Each source step reframes around that mesh centroid with bounds-based camera distance and a short visual transition. The same smooth deformation field acts on the source and its moving camera target. Backtracking and breadcrumbs retain the chosen descendant route. A source selector and child selectors provide keyboard alternatives to picking.

The first departure from the whole-lung view snapshots the actual camera yaw, pitch, distance, target and selected structure, including adjustments made before starting. **Original whole-lung view** restores that exact snapshot without changing simulation time, rate, depth, resistance, compliance, ventilation mode or playback. Rotation/zoom, source isolate, restore layers and touch rotation remain available. Touch defaults to vertical page scrolling; touch rotation/pinch requires its explicit toggle.

The quick parent/follow controls and current stage/provenance sit inside the scene frame so a continuous capture can follow the route. The full path, scale notes and learning controls follow the scene. The text fallback implements the same hierarchy, source boundary, route, scale notes and feedback without WebGL; the shared controller supplies its mechanics clock readout.

Observe → Predict → Reveal asks the learner to reconstruct the next structure or distinguish bulk airflow from barrier diffusion. Incorrect choices receive a specific explanation and remain retryable. These formative checks do not alter lesson completion, stored course progress, quiz order or the 100-question mock.

## Rendering and physiology limits

`explorer-detail.js` loads only when the learner first enters an authored distal level. It rebuilds a single bounded detail group when the level changes (maximum authored distal group 4,176 triangles in the current scene). It allocates no renderer. The pre-existing alveolar exchange geometry is now created only on entry to an alveolar level. Inactive detail is hidden and is disposed with the main scene, including late-load cancellation protection.

Cyan bulk-air particles travel within conducting lumens and into the alveolar region, reversing with mechanics flow. They do not cross the alveolar wall. Separate cyan O₂ and gold CO₂ particles cross the exaggerated alveolar–capillary barrier, following separate qualitative gradients; red cells remain red and within the capillary. The gas animation remains qualitative and does not solve transport, binding or saturation. Diffusion is independent of inspiration versus expiration.
