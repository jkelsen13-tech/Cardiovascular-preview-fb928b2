# Respiratory v2 model and geometry contract

## Numerical model

This is an independently implemented, linear single lung-compartment teaching model, evaluated after consulting the NSR/IMAG [Tidal_Human](https://www.imagwiki.nibib.nih.gov/physiome/jsim/models/webmodel/NSR/tidalhuman) equations on October 18, 2026. No NSR/JSim source code was reused. The candidate reference combines chest-wall and lung compliance and serial resistances with a sinusoidal pleural drive and anatomical volume offsets. This implementation deliberately uses one lung compliance, one airway resistance, relative reference volume, and a prescribed inspiratory pressure step. It is not a reproduction or validation of that whole model.

`V` is change in volume above an idealized relaxed reference, in L. It is not total lung volume, FRC, RV or TLC. `R` is airway resistance in cmH₂O·s/L; `C` is lung compliance in L/cmH₂O; `t` is physiological seconds. `Q = dV/dt = (D − V/C)/R`, positive inward. `R*C` has units of seconds. For constant drive over Δt the exact exponential solution is `Vnext = C*D + (V − C*D)*exp(−Δt/(R*C))`.

The breath period is `60/rate`; inspiration occupies 40%. Drive is a positive constant during inspiration and zero during quiet expiration. Pressure changes at phase boundaries are prescribed steps; volume remains continuous. They are not measured muscle activation kinetics. The choice ensures that actual flow, volume change and phase direction agree throughout the allowed R/C range. An initial ramp trial could allow early outward flow under large R*C; that trial was replaced rather than retaining inconsistent inspiration labels. The first checkpoint recording predates this refinement and final UI integration.

Integration uses the exact exponential propagator on two boundary-aligned grids, maximum step 1/240 s. A unit-drive cycle maps `Vnext = a*Vinitial + b`, where `a=exp(−period/(R*C))`. The periodic initial condition is `b/(1−a)`, avoiding startup drift. At any scrub position, the state is recomputed from the cached cycle and exact substep; backward/long-duration lookup does not integrate from the previous animation frame. The timestep test compares 1/120, 1/240 and 1/480 s grids; for constant phase drive the propagator is exact to floating-point tolerance. Independently specified closed-form constant-drive and flow-integral tests are also required.

Target mode scales unit-drive tidal excursion to the requested target and reports the required amplitude. Fixed-effort mode sets amplitude to `5*(depth/0.5)` cmH₂O and holds that amplitude when R/C change; delivered volume then changes. Rate range 6–30/min, depth 0.2–1 L, R 1–20 and C 0.04–0.2 are illustrative experiment bounds, not diagnostic ranges or treatment recommendations. Recalculation establishes a new periodic teaching state, rather than simulating a patient transition after a setting change.

All pressures are relative to ambient (0 cmH₂O). Baseline pleural pressure −5 cmH₂O and baseline elastic recoil +5 are illustrative. Spontaneous mode: airway opening `Pao=0`, `Ppl=−5−D`, `Palv=Ppl+5+V/C`. Positive-pressure comparison: `Pao=D`, `Ppl=−5`, `Palv=V/C`; passive displacement is not active diaphragmatic contraction. In both modes `Q=(Pao−Palv)/R`. Transpulmonary pressure is `Palv−Ppl`; trans-respiratory pressure is `Pao−body-surface pressure`, with body surface held at zero. The model does not separately solve chest-wall compliance or all pleural mechanics.

Animation receives state from this pure model. Wall-clock playback speed multiplies physiological elapsed time; it never changes rate/R/C. Hidden-page events pause simulation; pause is not called a biological breath-hold. The UI's volume, flow, pressure curves and markers read the same model. Synthetic capnography maps this period to the preserved source-labeled teaching phases. Its 40 mmHg endpoint remains a preset, not a mechanics-derived EtCO₂/PaCO₂/SpO₂ prediction.

## Geometry, registration and motion

The unmodified pinned HRA v1.2 reference remains separately available. `derive-breathing.py` reads its local wrapper, verifies the source hash, verifies that nodes have no nonidentity transforms, reads interleaved accessor strides correctly, and retains the full topology of selected source meshes. Source segments merge only by their lobe ancestry; selected named airway meshes merge into a separate airway layer. Dense bronchial cartilage and laryngeal cartilage are omitted, not decimated into unreliable branches. The derived six meshes total 54,594 triangles / 29,239 vertices. Exact members, hashes and the one shared center/scale transform are recorded in `respiratory-study/assets/breathing-provenance.json`.

Coordinates: +X patient left, +Y superior; +Z assumed anterior with educator orientation qualification pending. All selected anatomy shares the same transform: source center (−0.0049615577, 0.4785911739, −0.0801189294), scale 14.3474448326 to scene lung height 3.2. Physical meters are glTF convention, not independently measured. The six meshes are not independently centered or fitted. No male torso or isolated heart has been overlaid.

The authored smooth cage uses inferior position and distance from the upper airway/root region to weight outward/anterior and basal displacement. It displaces lung bases more than apices; it is neither group scaling nor a camera animation. The same field acts on the selected airways and label anchors. Picking projects current deformed triangles and selects frontmost depth; keyboard/select alternatives remain available. Cage displacement uses model volume excursion, but mesh volume has not been calibrated in liters. No local tissue stress or CFD claim is made.

Diaphragm and ribs are authored **schematic context**, not newly licensed anatomical meshes. They use this same HRA coordinate frame and lung extent landmarks. The resting diaphragm has ellipse radii 1.86/1.18, rim y=−2.25 and dome rise 0.51. It moves downward/flattens with positive volume excursion using coefficient 0.48, with radial expansion following the schematic chest. Schematic rib arcs surround the lungs (radii 2.4/1.65) and expand/lift with excursion. They are illustrative outlines, not all twelve anatomically shaped ribs, muscle fibers or a validated chest wall. Geometry tests and front/side captures check clearance at cycle extremes; detailed regional movement and source airway continuity remain educator-review limits. No invented lobar/segmental names are assigned to decorative branches.

The magnified rotatable 3D view is a separately authored airspace/barrier/capillary/red-cell/particle schematic. Cyan O₂ traverses air to blood; gold CO₂ traverses blood to air, each down its own gas partial-pressure gradient. Red cells remain shades of red. Motion is qualitative; there is no binding, transport or saturation solver. Exchange is not limited to expiration and O₂ is not shown converting into CO₂. The pulmonary route links to existing heart lessons without imposing one heartbeat per breath.
